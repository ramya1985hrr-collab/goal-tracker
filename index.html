04 — Engine

Run matching

The engine scores every student–internship pair on skill coverage, breadth of overlap, stated preference and CGPA, then runs a stable (deferred-acceptance) allocation so no student or internship would rather swap.

Scoring weights fixed for transparency

FactorWeightWhat it measures
Required-skill coverage60%Share of the internship's required skills the student has
Overlap breadth (Jaccard)20%How much the student's & role's skill sets overlap overall
Stated preference rank15%Bonus if the student ranked this internship highly
CGPA5%Small tiebreaker among similarly-matched students
